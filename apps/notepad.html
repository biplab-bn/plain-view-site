What it is

Not a bare text box. A notepad that reads and organises what you write.

It renders what you write

Markdown headings, bold, lists and tables, plus HTML, are rendered properly in reading mode. You keep writing plain text; the app shows you what you meant. Write an ordinary web address and it becomes a tappable link.

It organises without interrupting you

Tag a note by typing #hashtag anywhere in the text, or tap Tag to attach one without touching a single word you wrote. Filter the whole library by tag. Pin the notes you care about so they stay at the top.

It never loses an edit

Every keystroke is autosaved as a draft. Kill the app mid-sentence and pick up exactly where you left off, including a note you never saved.

Finding things is fast

Search across every note instantly, plus find and replace with case-insensitive matching.

Trash, not deletion

Removed notes stay for 30 days, with one-tap Undo. A stray tap does not lose anything.

Made for reading and writing

Choose a font and a size that suits you. Light, dark, or follow your system. Word, character and line counts as you type. Export to .txt, .md or .html, or print to paper or PDF.

Works on both sizes of screen

One pane on phones; the list and the editor side by side on tablets. Full keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+N, Ctrl+E, Ctrl+S, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+H, Ctrl+F.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no Android permissions at all — not even internet access, which means the app has no way to send anything anywhere even if it wanted to. Everything stays on your device. Your notes can be sent to and from any other app on your phone, and files can be opened straight from a file manager, but that always happens because you asked for it.
